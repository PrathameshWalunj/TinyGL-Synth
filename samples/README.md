## Spatial Reasoning 

![Spatial Reasoning Grid](spatial_reasoning_grid.png)

**5000 scenes × 10 QA pairs = 50,000 training examples in 3 seconds!**

Questions like:
- "How many red objects?" -> 2
- "Which color is highest?" -> yellow
- "More blue than green?" -> no
